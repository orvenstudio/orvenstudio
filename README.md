# ORVEN Studio

[English](README.md) | [한국어](README.ko.md)

> **New perspectives. Enduring value.**

ORVEN Studio is a technology and creative development studio focused on building thoughtful digital experiences, products, and systems.

---

## Brand Meaning

The name **ORVEN** is inspired by the idea of being **Original, yet Even**.

**Original** represents a distinctive perspective, curiosity, and the willingness to explore new possibilities.

**Even** does not simply mean balance or uniformity. It represents a considered, grounded, and consistent approach—one that does not pursue novelty for its own sake.

ORVEN reflects the belief that new ideas become meaningful when they remain connected to what truly matters.

---

## Brand Principle

### Original in perspective.  
### Considered in approach.  
### Enduring in value.

Each project is approached from a distinct perspective while remaining grounded in its purpose, users, and essential value.

The goal is to create work that is not only new or visually impressive, but thoughtful, useful, and valuable beyond the moment.

---

## What We Value

### Original Perspective

We question familiar assumptions and look for better ways to understand and solve problems.

### Thoughtful Execution

We make deliberate decisions based on purpose and context rather than trends or unnecessary complexity.

### Essential Quality

We focus on clarity, usability, structure, and the details that directly affect the experience.

### Enduring Value

We create products and systems that remain useful, meaningful, and adaptable beyond their initial release.
